# Focus Guard

A personal Android app that blocks distracting apps and websites while you study.

- **Focus time**: pick the minutes and start. Blocked apps, websites and Focus Guard's own settings stay locked until it ends. 2 emergency minutes every 20 minutes.
- **Quick start**: from the notification (30 / 45 / 60 min), the "Focus" Quick Settings tile, or by long-pressing the app icon.
- **Daily limits**: give apps a number of minutes per day. Once it's used up, the app is blocked until midnight.
- **Bedtime**: distracting apps are blocked between the times you set. 5 emergency minutes per hour.
- **Weekly lock**: daily limits and bedtime are locked for 7 days once saved. After that you can keep them for another week or change them.

## Getting the app
Every time the code changes, GitHub builds the app automatically. Open **Releases** and download `FocusGuard.apk` from the newest build. It installs over the old version and keeps your settings.

## First-time setup
1. Install `FocusGuard.apk` (allow "Install unknown apps"; if Play Protect blocks it, pause Play Protect while installing).
2. Open Focus Guard and allow notifications.
3. Tap **Accessibility** and turn on **Focus Guard**. If the switch is greyed out: tap **App info**, then ⋮, then **Allow restricted settings**, and try again.
